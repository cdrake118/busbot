# Research Stream 4: Less-Crowded Solopreneur Models — Directories, Lead Gen, API Products, Communities (2024–2026)

## 1. Niche Directories — real model, heavily course-inflated

**Key claims:**

- **John Rush (the face of the 2024 X/Twitter directory boom):** launched ~20 directories; **5 succeeded, ~15 failed or stalled** — a 25% hit rate from the person promoting the model. Successes (nextjsstarter.com, osssoftware.org, mvpwizards.com) reportedly reach ~$3–5k/mo each; one (AllGPTs.co) sold for a five-figure exit. His broader stated record: "I launched 30 projects, 20 failed, 10 survived." Sources: [Founderoo playbook](https://www.founderoo.co/playbooks/john-rush-marsxinc-directories), [his own X post](https://x.com/johnrushx/status/1761187622024585647) (Feb 2024), [Substack playbook](https://johnrushx.substack.com/p/directory-playbook-build-grow-and). **Confidence: plausible self-report — and he sells a $99 "Directory Guide," so direct financial incentive to inflate.** Notably, Rush frames directories partly as *marketing channels driving traffic to his premium SaaS tools*, not standalone businesses — a detail course buyers miss.
- **Frey Chu:** portfolio of directories in "unsexy" niches (porta-potty rentals, flea markets, liquidation stores, plasma donation) generating **$2,500–3,000/mo total**, ~60k monthly visits on the main site, first site took **~6 months to $1,200/mo profit**. Monetization: display ads (Mediavine/AdSense) → premium listings → selling leads in high-ticket niches. Sources: [Niche Pursuits interview](https://www.nichepursuits.com/frey-chu-directory-sites/) (2025), [Outscraper case study](https://outscraper.com/directory-guy-earns-2500-month-using-outscraper/). **Confidence: plausible self-report; he also sells a course ("Ship Your Directory") — flag.**
- **Failure base rate:** No systematic post-mortem data exists on the 2024 directory wave — itself a red flag (failures vanish silently). The promoter's own 25% success rate is the best available number. Meanwhile the macro headwind is severe: **60% of Google searches end with zero clicks (2025), 83% when an AI Overview appears**, and publisher organic traffic fell ~33% in the year to Nov 2025 ([TheDigitalBloom report](https://thedigitalbloom.com/learn/2025-organic-traffic-crisis-analysis-report/), [Pixelmojo](https://www.pixelmojo.io/blogs/google-traffic-dropped-33-percent-ai-search-shift), [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-overviews-surge-pullback-data-466314)). Directories that depend on informational SEO traffic + display ads are directly in the blast radius. Transactional/local "near me" queries are less affected.

**Verdict:** Real but small. Outcomes cluster at $0; the documented wins are $1–5k/mo per site after 6–12 months, with lead-gen monetization (not ads/listings) as the only durable path. The "$20k/mo directory" claims are course-marketing tail outcomes.

## 2. Local Lead Gen / Rank-and-Rent — viable economics, rising platform risk

- **Cost-per-lead benchmarks (2025):** legal ~$320/lead, financial services ~$270, healthcare ~$285, real estate ~$220; home-services contractor leads typically far lower but sites rent for **$500–$3,000/mo flat** ([Flyweel CPL Benchmark Index 2025](https://www.flyweel.co/blog/lead-gen-cpl-cac-benchmark-index-2025), [Post Affiliate Pro pricing guide](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/faq/how-much-to-pay-per-lead/)). **Confidence: verified-ish industry benchmarks (ad-buy CPL, not what a solo site nets — your sell price will be below these).**
- **Viability:** practitioner consensus is "still works in low-competition local niches, but harder" ([CMO Eugene](https://www.cmoeugene.com/rank-rent-still-works-in-2025/), [BlackHatWorld thread](https://www.blackhatworld.com/seo/is-the-rank-and-rent-model-still-viable-in-2025.1727675/)). **Confidence: plausible self-report; ippei.com and similar sources are course funnels — flag.**
- **The serious pitfalls (verified):**
  - **GBP crackdown:** suspensions up ~80% (Q1'23–Q2'24) and ~40% again in 2025; video verification now standard; Google filed a federal suit (March 2025, *Google v. Asayag*) against a network of **10,000+ fake lead-gen listings** in HVAC/appliance/locksmith niches. Practitioners now explicitly advise against building new lead-gen businesses on Maps listings ([Asset Digital](https://assetdigitalcom.com/blog/google-business-profile-suspension-2025/), [Appliance Marketing Pros](https://appliancemarketingpros.com/blog/googles-gbp-crackdown/), [Sterling Sky](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/top-reasons-google-my-business-suspended-your-listing/)). **Confidence: verified.**
  - **Regulatory:** the FCC's one-to-one consent rule was **vacated by the 11th Circuit (Jan 2025)** — a reprieve for lead resellers — but TCPA prior-express-consent rules still apply and the consent-revocation rule took effect April 2025; FTC fake-review penalties run to $50k/incident ([McGuireWoods](https://www.mcguirewoods.com/client-resources/alerts/2025/1/delayed-one-to-one-consent-rule-gives-companies-reprieve-plus-other-tcpa-updates/), [MoFo](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/250130-eleventh-circuit-vacates-fcc-s-tcpa-one-to-one-consent-rule)). **Confidence: verified.**

**Verdict:** The website-based (not GBP-based) version, selling phone/form leads to high-ticket local services ($500+ jobs), remains real: one rented site at $1–2k/mo is achievable in 4–9 months in weak-competition metros. GBP-spam variants are now actively litigated against — avoid.

## 3. API & Data Products — quietly real, but the marketplace channel died

- **RapidAPI collapsed:** once valued at $1B with 4M users / 40k APIs, it declined to "thousands" of users and "hundreds" of APIs before Nokia bought the assets (Nov 13, 2024) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/13/nokia-acquires-rapid-the-api-company-once-valued-at-1b/), [Nokia](https://www.nokia.com/newsroom/nokia-acquires-rapid-technology-and-rd-unit-to-strengthen-development-of-network-api-solutions-and-ecosystem/)). **Confidence: verified.** The "list an API on a marketplace and collect checks" play is effectively dead; distribution must be your own (SEO, dev communities).
- **Direct-sold APIs/micro-SaaS still work:** indie playbook data suggests 60–70% of revenue from the middle pricing tier and that 20 customers at $500/mo beats 200 at $50 ([Zuplo monetization playbook](https://zuplo.com/learning-center/from-zero-to-1m-mrr-api-monetization-indie-hackers), 2025). Median **profitable** micro-SaaS ≈ $4.2k MRR; ~70% never clear $1k MRR; ~30% abandoned before $1k ([Freemius State of Micro-SaaS 2025](https://freemius.com/blog/state-of-micro-saas-2025/), [SoftwareSeni metrics](https://www.softwareseni.com/solo-founder-saas-metrics-from-0-to-10k-mrr-in-6-months-with-realistic-timelines/)). **Confidence: directional/plausible — vendor-published aggregates.**
- Outlier proof of ceiling: Pieter Levels' Photo AI ($132k/mo at month 18, 87% margins) and Nomad List ($5.3M in 2024) are real but singular, audience-powered cases ([appkodes roundup](https://appkodes.com/blog/one-person-indie-saas-projects-built-using-ai/)). **Confidence: plausible self-report (Levels publishes open dashboards).**

**Verdict:** Real, but it's just micro-SaaS with an API skin. Data products (scraped/curated datasets sold as API or CSV subscriptions) have thin documented 2024–26 evidence at solo scale — mostly sold as enrichment for the directory/lead-gen models above (e.g., Outscraper's ecosystem).

## 4. Paid Communities (Skool) & Job Boards — winner-take-most

- **Skool reality vs. hype:** of 2,600+ analyzed communities, **~70% are free (earn $0)**. Among *paid* ones, median MRR ≈ **$2,800–4,200**, modal outcome ~$5k MRR (120–260 members at $19–39/mo), top decile >$25k; churn floor 6–9%/mo caps growth ([Communipass benchmarks](https://communipass.com/blog/skool-revenue-benchmarks-2026/), [Joe Apfelbaum's top-1000 analysis](https://joeapfelbaum.substack.com/p/skool-communities-analysis-top-1000), [Codingoblin 2,600-group data](https://codingoblin.com/blog/most-profitable-skool-community-niches)). **Confidence: plausible — third-party scrapes of public Skool data, but published by sites selling Skool tooling.** Independent reviews flag the Hormozi/affiliate-driven hype, "pump-and-dump MRR" incentives from the Skool Games leaderboard, and note the leaderboard only showcases winners ([courseplatformsreview](https://www.courseplatformsreview.com/blog/skool-games/), [withhimanshu](https://withhimanshu.com/skool-games-review/)). A paid community only works downstream of an existing audience — it is not a cold-start model.
- **Job boards:** RemoteOK reportedly ~$35k/mo solo-operated at 600–800k visits, $599+ listings; Work With Indies hit $5k MRR after building community first; niche-board benchmarks: **$300–600 per posting** where the audience is hard to reach, featured upsells converting 15–25% ([Cavuno monetization guide](https://cavuno.com/blog/job-board-monetization), 2025/26). **Confidence: RemoteOK plausible (Levels publishes numbers); benchmarks directional.** The chicken-and-egg problem is the documented killer; no recent candid job-board post-mortems surfaced, which again means failures go unrecorded. Also exposed to the 2025–26 white-collar hiring slowdown and AI-spam applications devaluing postings.

**Verdict:** Both are audience-first businesses. Median paid community ≈ $3–4k/mo *conditional on charging at all*; median job board ≈ $0 until you own the niche's attention.

## 5. Other models — what the 2024–26 record actually supports

- **AI tools for specific professions:** strongest fresh evidence. Vertical pricing power is real: legal AI $500–2,000/mo per customer, healthcare intake $600–1,500/mo, real estate $400–1,000/mo ([Medium/CodeOrbit data roundup](https://medium.com/@theabhishek.040/solo-developers-building-100k-1m-revenue-micro-saas-2024-110838470a2a)); a niche AI calculator case study claims $0→$50k MRR with 5 enterprise customers worth $4.5k MRR ([Estha case study](https://estha.ai/blog/case-study-how-a-solo-founder-scaled-from-0-to-50k-mrr-with-a-niche-ai-calculator/)). **Confidence: plausible-to-promotional — Estha sells an AI-builder platform.** Distribution stats: 36% of new 2025 startups are solo-founded ([Carta](https://carta.com/data/solo-founders-report/), verified).
- **Productized services:** documented range $74k/yr (solo) to $3.6M/yr; e.g., MixBloom white-label social content $199–549/mo per client ([ManyRequests: 15 success stories](https://manyrequests.com/blog/productized-service-revenue)). **Confidence: plausible self-reports.** Fastest route to first revenue of anything on this list (weeks, not months).
- **Translation/localization: AVOID.** This is the clearest negative finding of the whole review: post-editing rates halved (€0.04→€0.02/word), individual translators reporting 60–80% income collapses, 36% of UK translators lost work to genAI by 2024 ([Brian Merchant / Blood in the Machine](https://www.bloodinthemachine.com/p/ai-killed-my-job-translators), [Washington Post, Sept 2025](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/09/26/ai-translation-jobs/), [Slator 2025](https://slator.com/five-ways-ai-reshaped-translation-industry-2025/)). **Confidence: verified.** Entering as a solopreneur in 2026 means selling into a deflating market.

**Cross-cutting adversarial note:** nearly every positive number in sections 1, 2, and 4 originates from someone monetizing the model's popularity (Rush's $99 guide, Chu's "Ship Your Directory," ippei.com's coaching funnel, Skool's $99/mo + affiliate army, Communipass's Skool tools). No source publishes failure-rate panels; the only honest base rates available are Rush's own 25% directory hit rate, Skool's 70%-free statistic, and micro-SaaS's ~70%-below-$1k-MRR distribution. Assume the median outcome of any of these is $0 and the documented "typical success" is $1–5k/mo.

## Bottom line for a 2-person team targeting $100k year-one portfolio revenue

**Realistic core (build around these):**
1. **AI-powered vertical tool for one profession** (legal, healthcare admin, trades back-office) — only model here with documented solo paths past $10k MRR and pricing power ($300–2,000/mo per customer). Slowest to start, highest ceiling.
2. **Productized/unbundled agency service** in that same vertical — fastest cash ($2–10k/mo within 1–3 months), funds the rest, and doubles as customer discovery for #1.
3. **2–3 lead-gen directories in high-ticket local niches** (website-based, selling leads/renting, NOT GBP listings, NOT display ads) — expect $1–2.5k/mo each at month 6–12, with maybe 1 in 3–4 attempts working. Treat as a portfolio bet and a distribution asset, not a primary income.

**Plausible add-on:** a paid community or job board **only after** one of the above gives you an audience; budget median expectations of $3–5k/mo, not leaderboard numbers.

**Avoid:** API marketplaces (RapidAPI is dead), GBP-based rank-and-rent (active Google litigation + suspension wave), translation/localization (verified income collapse), ad-monetized informational directories (AI Overviews are eating the traffic), and buying any $99–$999 course about the above — the courses are where the model's promoters actually make their money.

**Math check:** vertical tool at $4k MRR by month 12 (~$25k year-one cumulative) + productized service at $6k/mo from month 3 (~$60k) + two working directories averaging $1.5k/mo in H2 (~$15k) ≈ $100k. Achievable, but only if the service revenue carries the first six months — no model on this list reliably produces meaningful passive income inside 6 months.
