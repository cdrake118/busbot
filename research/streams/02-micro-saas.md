# Research Stream 2: Micro-SaaS / Solo-Founder Reality Check, 2024–2026 Evidence

## 1. Real revenue distributions (not survivor stories)

**Claim: Median Indie Hackers Stripe-verified product makes ~$169/month; >half make essentially nothing.**
- Analysis of 5,079 Stripe-verified indie projects (TrustMRR data): median monthly revenue $169; only ~0.9% (48 projects) qualify as "breaking out" (>$10k/mo and growing fast). Source: [indiehackers.com — "I analyzed 5,079 Stripe-verified startups"](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-analyzed-5-079-stripe-verified-startups-f0f6bd053f) (2025) and [onemilliongoal.com](https://www.onemilliongoal.com/p/what-986-solo-startups-actually-look). Confidence: **verified revenue, but selection-biased toward people who publicly share** — the true population distribution is worse.
- Earlier ScrapingFish analysis of 937 Stripe-verified IH products: ~54% make $0; ~5% exceed ~$8.3k/mo ($100k/yr). [scrapingfish.com/blog/indie-hackers-revenue](https://scrapingfish.com/blog/indie-hackers-revenue) (2022). Confidence: verified-but-dated; consistent with the newer data.
- A contradictory stat ("44% of TrustMRR projects make $1k+/mo") exists, but TrustMRR over-samples Marc Lou's community of established builders. Confidence: **plausible but heavily survivor-biased**.

**Claim: ~70% of micro-SaaS sit under $1k MRR; only 1–2% exceed $50k/mo.**
- Aggregations citing MicroConf's [State of Independent SaaS survey](https://microconf.com/state-of-indie-saas) (1,000+ businesses): median micro-SaaS ~$500 MRR; 70% under $1k MRR; 18% in $1k–5k. Via [rockingweb.com.au](https://www.rockingweb.com.au/micro-saas-revenue-analysis-2025/) and [saasranger.com](https://saasranger.com/blog/micro-saas-revenue-reality-what-1000-founders-actually-earn/) (2025). Confidence: **plausible secondhand aggregation — these are content-marketing blogs repackaging MicroConf/Freemius data; directionally consistent with Stripe-verified data but treat exact percentages cautiously.**

**Claim (highest quality data point): Stripe's own 2025 solo-founder data shows the median getting worse while the top gets better.**
- [Stripe blog, "Solo founding is at an all-time high"](https://stripe.com/blog/top-solo-founder-traits) (2025): median first-six-month revenue of solo-founded Atlas startups **down 23% YoY in 2025**; top decile **up 19%**; the top-decile-to-median gap widened from **34x (2021) to 61x (2025)**. Solo-founder share of new startups rose 23.7% (2019) → 36.3% (mid-2025). Confidence: **verified, first-party payments data**. This is the single best evidence on the question: more people are trying, the median outcome is shrinking, winners take more.

**Exit-market data (Acquire.com / Flippa):**
- Acquire.com: SaaS sold at **median 3.9x TTM profit in both 2024 and 2025**; typical margins ~71%; only ~45% of startups pass listing curation at all. [Acquire.com Biannual Multiples Report, Jan 2026](https://blog.acquire.com/acquire-com-biannual-acquisition-multiples-report-jan-2026/); [annual report PDF](https://blog.acquire.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/acquire-annual-report-2025.pdf); [Investors Club stats](https://investors.club/acquire-statistics/). Confidence: **verified marketplace data**.
- Flippa: small deals get small multiples — median profit multiples **1.68x for $10k–100k deals**, 1.96x ($100k–500k), 2.43x ($1M+). [flippa.com/blog/saas-multiples](https://flippa.com/blog/saas-multiples/) (2025–2026). Confidence: verified marketplace data. Implication: a $2k-MRR micro-SaaS is worth maybe $30–60k at exit — the "build small, flip" math is thin.

## 2. Time-to-revenue benchmarks

- **First dollar: median ~3–4 months** from start of development (MicroConf/Freemius-derived aggregations, 2025). Confidence: plausible self-report.
- **$1k MRR: median 12–18 months** for those who get there; **$5k MRR: 2–4 years** "for most founders who get there at all." Via [saasranger.com](https://saasranger.com/blog/micro-saas-revenue-reality-what-1000-founders-actually-earn/) citing MicroConf data. Confidence: plausible; matches verified anecdotes (below).
- Verified anchor: **Tally** (2-person team) took ~1.5 years to $10k MRR and ~3 years to $1M ARR — and they're an unusually successful outcome. [blog.tally.so](https://blog.tally.so/our-bootstrapped-journey-towards-100k-mrr/) (Feb 2024). Confidence: **verified founder-published**.
- Beware content-farm "benchmarks" like "$10K MRR in 6 months with realistic timelines" (softwareseni.com) and fictional-sounding case studies ("Sarah Chen built ScreenshotAPI") — **likely hype/AI-generated filler**.

## 4. AI commoditization risk — both directions

**Harder (commoditization is real):**
- Documented wrapper die-off: foundation models absorb wrapper features on a ~2–3 year cycle; the ["Killed by AI" graveyard](https://mixtpatrik.github.io/killedbyai/) tracks ~90 dead AI products; analysis at [machinebrief.com](https://www.machinebrief.com/news/death-of-ai-wrapper-startups-wont-survive-2026) and [dev.to AI graveyard](https://dev.to/dev_tips/the-graveyard-of-ai-startups-startups-that-forgot-to-build-real-value-5ad9) (2025–2026). Confidence: plausible synthesis, consistent with Marc Lou's and others' revenue declines.
- "Build cost dropped 90%+; the cost of getting noticed did not" — Product Hunt is flooded with near-identical AI launches ([WebDesigner Depot](https://medium.com/@WebdesignerDepot/how-producthunt-com-became-overrun-with-ai-products-3ae12b948b22), 2024–2025). Stripe's 61x top-decile gap (verified, above) is the quantitative signature of this.
- Vibe-coding failure modes are documented, not hypothetical: **Enrichlead** (built "100% with Cursor, zero handwritten code") was breached within days of launch, March 2025; Tenzai's Dec 2025 audit of 15 AI-built production apps found 69 vulnerabilities, every app missing CSRF protection. [getautonoma.com/blog/vibe-coding-failures](https://getautonoma.com/blog/vibe-coding-failures), [CSA research note](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-ai-generated-code-security-vibe-coding-202/). Confidence: verified incidents.

**Easier (for the right product):**
- Freemius's [2025 State of Micro-SaaS](https://freemius.com/blog/state-of-micro-saas-2025/): 45.7% of their SaaS makers are solo; 68% of successful solo founders use AI for development acceleration (not as the product); AI absorbs overhead that once forced hiring. Confidence: plausible platform-biased survey. (Their "95% reach profitability in year one" stat should be discounted — Freemius only sees products that got far enough to monetize on their platform.)
- Defensible niches per 2025–2026 analyses ([Advisable](https://www.advisable.com/insights/micro-saas-2026-viable-strategy-or-eaten-alive-by-ai-feature-bloat), [Vendep](https://www.vendep.com/post/forget-the-data-moat-the-workflow-is-your-fortress-in-vertical-saas)): workflow embeddedness in "boring" verticals (accumulated templates, logged transactions, integrations into the vertical's toolstack), compliance/regulated niches, and community trust. PE did a record 73 vertical-SaaS transactions worth $15.3B in Q1 2025. Confidence: plausible; the moat logic is sound, the "7–10x ARR premium for bootstrapped vertical SaaS" figure is **likely hype**.

## 5. Distribution-first marketplace plays (2024–2026 data)

- **Shopify App Store: the best risk-adjusted marketplace.** ~11,900 apps (Nov 2024, only ~257 added/month — low flood rate vs. AI tools); devs keep **100% of first $1M lifetime revenue** (post-Jan 2025 rules); ecosystem paid devs $230M+ in 2024. Claimed "average dev revenue ~$93k/yr" is from a stats roundup ([uptek.com](https://uptek.com/shopify-statistics/app-store/)) — **treat as upper-bound/selection-biased**, but real merchants pay real monthly fees for boring ops problems. [shopify.dev revenue share](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share). Confidence: rules verified; earnings averages plausible-to-hype.
- **Chrome extensions: brutal distribution lottery.** Median extension has **~17 installs**; ~87% have <1,000 installs; ~10% have zero users; the vast majority earn $0. [DebugBear Chrome extension statistics](https://www.debugbear.com/blog/chrome-extension-statistics) (2024), [chrome-stats.com](https://chrome-stats.com/chrome/stats). Confidence: **verified scrape data**. (Ignore Starter Story's "average successful extension makes $72.8k/month" — classic survivorship hype.)
- **WordPress plugins: low ceiling, consolidating.** CodeCanyon average plugin: ~$3.8k/yr gross (~$224/mo net); standard free→paid conversion 1–3%; many devs plateau at $3–5k/mo; 2024–25 consolidation wave (Awesome Motive, WP Engine buying up plugins). [Freemius CodeCanyon analysis](https://freemius.com/blog/codecanyon-wordpress-plugins-analysis/), [mktclarity.com](https://mktclarity.com/blogs/news/wordpress-plugin-worth-it). Confidence: verified platform data (dated) + plausible commentary.
- **Notion templates: was a gold rush (2021–2023), now saturated.** Easlo: $500k+ cumulative, ~$20k/mo at 2022 peak; Thomas Frank reportedly ~$1M. Typical active creators now $500–3k/mo. [Medium Easlo case study](https://medium.com/@hey.musli/case-study-1-easlo-21-years-old-earned-over-500k-from-selling-notion-templates-bdf5f086ab43). Confidence: plausible self-reports, peak-era numbers; expect worse today.

## Bottom line: 2-person technical team, $100k year-one portfolio revenue

**Verdict: not realistic as a base case from products alone; achievable only as a top-5–10% outcome, and only with a distribution-first plan.** $100k in year one means exiting the year around $12–15k MRR (ramping). The verified base rates: median Stripe-verified indie product is $169/mo; median time to $1k MRR is 12–18 months; even Tally — a celebrated 2-person success — took ~18 months to reach $10k MRR. Stripe's own data shows the median first-six-month revenue *fell 23% in 2025* while the top decile pulled away (61x gap). Everyone who hit your target fast either had an audience, an ad-spend playbook, or first-mover timing. A more honest year-one target for a strong 2-person team with no audience: **$20–50k total revenue from products, with $100k+ ARR run-rate as a year-two goal.**

**Best sub-plays, ranked:**
1. **Boring B2B vertical workflow tool** (one industry, deep integrations, compliance-adjacent): slowest start, only durable moat for a tiny team, best exit multiples (Acquire.com 3.9x profit; vertical premium).
2. **Shopify app(s)**: built-in buyer intent, 100% of first $1M revenue, lowest flood rate of the marketplaces. Pair one "review/ops" app with fast iteration on merchant reviews.
3. **Rondot-style fast-follow portfolio with paid ads**: clone validated categories, Google/Meta ads from week one, programmatic SEO, kill anything not converting in 60–90 days. Proven to ~$35k/mo solo, but requires ad budget, marketing skill, and tolerance for churn-and-burn; weakest moat.
4. **Audience-first (build in public)**: works (Dinh, Lou, Postma) but is a 1–3 year pre-investment, not a year-one play.

**Avoid:** thin GPT wrappers (documented 2–3 year absorption cycle), Chrome extensions as a primary bet (median 17 installs), Notion templates (post-peak), and shipping vibe-coded products without a security pass (Enrichlead-class breaches are now routine).
