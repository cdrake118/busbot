# Naming Shortlist & Conflict Screen

*DNS screening done in-session (June 2026); conflict screening by research
agent (~34 searches). Final picks belong to Cory/Austin. Before purchase:
manual registrar check (a "no DNS" domain can be registered-but-dormant) and
a proper USPTO TESS clearance — TESS blocked automated search.*

## Service brand

| Name | Domain | Conflict risk | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BackBinder** (recommended) | backbinder.com (likely available) | **LOW — cleanest screened** | Zero in-space collisions; only matches are medical back braces and UK stationery. "Binder" is a native insurance term (a binder *is* a temporary insurance contract) — insiders will get it instantly. |
| **Ninety Sixty Thirty** | ninetysixtythirty.com (likely available) | LOW collision / MEDIUM protectability | The renewal-cadence brand; memorable, instantly communicates the renewal engine. Risks: descriptive (90/60/30 is generic industry convention, hard to trademark); adjacency to Ninety (ninety.com, insurance consultancy). |
| PolicyDesk | policydesk.com (no DNS, unverified) | MEDIUM — **on hold** | Best generic name, but: policydesk.co is an active software product (GDPR plugin), "Policy Desk" is an insurance seller in India, and getpolicydesk.com is owned by an unidentified party that blocks crawlers. Needs manual WHOIS + browser visit before consideration. |
| ~~AgencyDesk~~ | — | **HIGH — disqualified** | AgentDesk (agentdesk.net) is an AMS for small independent agencies; Agent Desk VA sells insurance back-office services — our exact category under a near-identical name; agencydesk.io holds the LinkedIn handle. |

## Newsletter brand

| Name | Domain | Conflict risk | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Premium & Loss** (recommended) | premiumandloss.com (likely available) | **LOW — pun unclaimed** | No insurance newsletter/podcast/blog uses the P&L pun. Distinctive, protectable, and perfectly matches the benchmarks/unit-economics positioning ("the operator's P&L letter"). |
| The Ops Letter | theopsletter.com (likely available) | LOW | Clean but generic; nothing in insurance uses it. Solid fallback. |
| Hard Market Letter | hardmarketletter.com (likely available) | LOW collision / strategic concern | Unclaimed but maximally descriptive, and the name dates badly when the market softens (industry press says softening has begun). Pass. |
| ~~The Book Letter~~ | — | MEDIUM — avoid | "The BookLetter" is an active multi-thousand-subscriber literary Substack; SEO/handle collisions guaranteed. |
| ~~Main Street Premium~~ | — | MEDIUM-HIGH — disqualified | Main Street America Insurance is a major P&C carrier distributed through our exact audience; implies affiliation. |

## Recommended pairing

**BackBinder** (the service) + **Premium & Loss** (the newsletter).

They work together: BackBinder is the quiet back-office brand agencies hire;
Premium & Loss is the independent editorial voice that builds the audience.
Distinct names keep the newsletter's "no vendor agenda" credibility while the
service sponsors/authors it transparently.
