## Sampling methodology

- **Sources:** web search for "independent insurance agency family owned [city]" in mid-size cities (not top metros), cross-referenced with Trusted Choice agency-profile pages, state Big "I" directory listings (e.g., flindependentagents.com), and carrier appointment pages.
- **States (10):** 4 hard-market (FL, TX, CA, LA) + 6 others (NC, OH, PA, IL, GA, WI), 5 agencies each. Cities: Lakeland/Ocala/Pensacola; Waco/Lubbock/Tyler; Fresno/Bakersfield/Modesto; Lafayette/Shreveport-Bossier/Houma; Hickory/Wilmington; Dayton/Canton; Lancaster/Erie; Peoria/Rockford; Macon/Savannah; Appleton/Eau Claire.
- **Exclusions applied:** captive agents (State Farm/Allstate/Farmers/American Family), national brokers and roll-ups (INSURICA, HUB, Higginbotham, Inszone, Ansay, Leavitt), franchise/SEO lead-gen networks (notably "Allen Thomas Group," which publishes identical city pages in every metro), and one agency-network subsidiary.
- **Verification:** direct HTTP fetch is blocked in this environment, so 45 of 50 domains were confirmed live via domain-restricted index searches (returning real deep URLs like `/request-a-quote/`); the remaining 5 via direct-snippet searches naming the domain. Spot-check URLs manually during batch prep.
